Signal Processing / Sensor Fusion / Time Synchronization

Visual–inertial time synchronization

Accuracy and efficiency across interpolation, cross-correlation, and phase fitting

Studies asynchronous camera and IMU sampling through four interpolation methods and two delay-estimation paths: time-domain cross-correlation and frequency-domain phase slope. Evaluates resampling rate, window length, error, and computational cost, combining coarse time-domain estimation with frequency-domain refinement for large delays, and examining detrending, Hann windows, and FFT acceleration.

Computer Vision / Image Segmentation / Transformer

Active contour segmentation

From Canny edge priors to Transformer-guided semantic contours

Evaluates accuracy, robustness, and runtime on complex BSDS500 scenes. Improves Chan–Vese segmentation with Canny edge priors, then introduces SAM / Transformer semantic masks in the CiSTN model to compare boundary extraction and convergence efficiency.

Optimal Control / MPC / Game Theory

My Gain Is Not Your Loss

Stochastic MPC and game theory for sports franchise management

Models basketball franchise management as a constrained, rolling-horizon decision problem. Combines stochastic MPC, risk-aware Monte Carlo tree search and a bi-level game to study roster selection, budget allocation, ticket pricing and injury response.

Renewable Energy / Time Series / Machine Learning

Multi-scale photovoltaic forecasting

Combining physical models with data-driven power prediction

Builds a physical baseline from solar position, plane-of-array irradiance, and temperature correction, and uses SARIMA to model periodicity. Investigates day-ahead power forecasts across seasons and weather conditions through weather-based grouping, XGBoost residual correction, and random-forest downscaling.

Atmospheric Modeling / XGBoost / VAR

Atmospheric boundary-layer forecasting

Combining XGBoost, VAR, and Bayesian information fusion

Uses lidar and meteorological observations from Jiaxing to study nonlinear drivers of boundary-layer height with XGBoost. Applies PCA and VAR to its temporal relationship with air quality, then combines model predictions through Bayesian information fusion.

Statistical Modeling / Risk Optimization / XGBoost

NIPT timing and risk modeling

Grouped fitting and risk penalties for selecting NIPT testing weeks

Analyzes the relationships among gestational age, BMI, and Y-chromosome concentration using testing data. Studies which gestational weeks to select for NIPT through grouped fitting, clustering, and risk-penalized objectives, with differential evolution and XGBoost residual correction. Also includes aneuploidy classification and sensitivity analysis.

Spatial Analysis / Regression / Clustering

New York Airbnb market analysis

Spatial patterns and pricing optimization for short-term rentals

Segments New York short-term rental listings through data cleaning, a PCA-based popularity index, and K-means clustering. Uses regression, Moran’s I, and General G to examine spatial differences in price and occupancy, then discusses pricing strategies under revenue objectives.

Sustainable Systems / Multi-objective Optimization / AHP

From Juno to Global

A multi-objective approach to sustainable tourism

Builds an economic, environmental and social evaluation framework for sustainable tourism in Juneau. Uses AHP weighting and simulated annealing to explore visitor and tax policies, then extends the model to Venice with fuzzy-coefficient linear programming and cross-destination visitor allocation.
