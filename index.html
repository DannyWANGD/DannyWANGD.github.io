A little about me

Hello! I’m Ding Wang, a Robotics Engineering undergraduate in the 2024 cohort of the Li Zexiang Innovation and Entrepreneurship Class at Teli College, Beijing Institute of Technology. My research interests include embodied AI, robot manipulation, reinforcement learning, and generative models. I focus on combining algorithms with hardware, and share what I learn through research notes and open-source projects.

I received a National Scholarship in 2026 and have been awarded first-class university scholarships on three consecutive occasions. In 2026, I served as the lead student investigator on a project under the Beijing Natural Science Foundation’s Undergraduate Research Initiation Program, with RMB 50,000 in funding. I have received eight competition awards at the national level or above, including a ROBOCON National First Prize, a Challenge Cup National Second Prize, a CUMCM National Second Prize, and an MCM/ICM Meritorious Winner award (M Award). I have also led two university-level student innovation projects and one national/municipal-level student innovation project.

My student leadership work includes developing and operating the Innovation Practice Base for the Li Zexiang Innovation and Entrepreneurship Class, and organizing class activities such as innovation-focused practical activities in Shenzhen and field visits to study embodied AI in Hangzhou. My honors include a university-level Outstanding Student award, a college-level Outstanding Youth League Member award, and an Outstanding Peer Mentor award, reflecting my development across academic work, research, and student leadership.

Embedded Robotics / STM32 / FreeRTOS

ROBOCON 2026

Robotics Competition Project

Embedded control for a volleyball robot’s front controller

Front-controller firmware for a ROBOCON volleyball robot, built with STM32F427, HAL, and FreeRTOS. Implements trajectory generation, kinematic computation, and torque commands for a three-motor striking platform, with cascaded PID for a two-axis gimbal, coordinated chassis commands, and CAN / UART DMA communication.

Research Tools / LLM Workflows / Obsidian

PaperBrain

Personal Open-source Project

An Obsidian-first research assistant that turns arXiv and Hugging Face papers into screened digests, in-depth reading notes, and research briefs. Pairs a Python analysis pipeline with an Obsidian console for setup, run control, and browsing results.

Human-Robot Interaction / Python / WebSocket

Focus Lamp

Internship Project

A focus-assistance lamp with light and motion feedback

A desktop interaction prototype built on Human Computer Lab’s open-source LeLamp platform. Adjusts color temperature and brightness using session time, fatigue level, and focus mode, and triggers motion feedback from external concentration ratings. Supports WebSocket parameter updates, motion recording and playback, and a standalone voice interface.
